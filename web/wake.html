How it is computed

The same C code as ./current-swimmer --karman, compiled to WebAssembly: a staggered grid, MacCormack advection, a pressure solve to 1 × 10⁻⁴ U/D and a volume-penalised post. The force includes the removed momentum and the pressure impulse on the post.

Limits

The browser uses 6 cells per diameter (the checks of make test); the handbook measures 6 to 16. The channel walls block 1/8 of the width. At Re 100 the solid-aware projection and a larger domain reduce the measured drag; its value still depends on the wall shape and resolution. The often-quoted open-water drag has not been checked against its original source. Above Re about 190 a real wake turns three-dimensional; this one cannot.
